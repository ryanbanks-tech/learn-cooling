How does your air conditioner actually make cold?

Your AC doesn't create cold — it moves heat. The same refrigerant loops around forever, changing pressure and state to carry heat from inside your home to the outdoors. Click any stage in the loop below to see what's happening.

The big idea

A home air conditioner is a heat pump. A special fluid called refrigerant runs in a sealed loop, and at every corner of that loop it's deliberately squeezed, cooled, expanded, or warmed so it can pick up heat in one place and drop it off in another. Repeat thousands of times an hour and your living room gets cold.
